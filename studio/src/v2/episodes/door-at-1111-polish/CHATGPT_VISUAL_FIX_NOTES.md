# Targeted visual logic fixes — review branch

Source branch: `v2-local-render`. Proposed changes isolated on `v2-visual-logic-fixes-chatgpt`. Original working branch and audio files are unchanged.

## Implemented source changes

- Sun: large distant sky sphere, with a fade in opacity rather than sinking below the roofline.
- Street: lower overlapping episode road surface; offset crossing asphalt layers to reduce z-fighting.
- Streetlights: move seven secondary lamps to alternating curbside positions along the road toward Latte's house; point the last-light camera at the actual last lamp.
- Bus stop: transparent back and side panels with supporting frames.
- Latte's home: more persistent window-glass visibility and dimmed interior moon fill on blackout.
- Café alley: narrow walled service passage, with existing service detail, props and passage preserved.
- Mouse chase: an original low-poly mouse actor leads Latte from the street into the alley before the power failure; actor path remains continuous into the homeward run.
- Alley movement: the mouse briefly reappears during the mysterious alley movement beat.
- Window view: cafe approach begins inside Latte's home and pushes toward the café through the window; third-tap shots maintain the house window perspective.
- Door staging: do not leave Mocha's physical blanket obstructing the café door during the second mysterious opening.
- Camera impact: stronger but short, localized door-opening shake.
