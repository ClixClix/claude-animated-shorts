In this town, every time a cat lies, their shadow grows.

A small lie adds an inch.
A big lie can cover a wall.
And one afternoon, Essie's shadow grew taller than the rooftops.

It started with a mug.

Mocha's favorite blue mug, to be exact.

Essie was cleaning the café when her tail knocked it off the counter.

Crash.

The mug shattered. A sharp piece nicked her paw.

Essie swept the pieces behind the register just as Mocha came in.

"Have you seen my mug?"

Essie tucked her scratched paw behind her back.

"No."

Her shadow stretched across the floor.

Mocha glanced at the empty shelf.

"Are you sure?"

"Maybe Latte took it."

Now her shadow climbed the wall.
Its ears reached the ceiling.

Essie pulled the blinds shut.
The shadow spread across the fabric.

She switched off the lights.

Outside, the sun caught her silhouette in the window.

So she ran.

On the sidewalk, her shadow shot up the café wall.

Across the street, Latte dropped his sandwich.

Two enormous ears rose above the rooftops.
A giant paw moved across the houses.

Cats screamed and scattered.

Essie ducked into the narrow alley, but the enormous shape followed.

She pressed herself against the wall.

Then someone called her name.

It was Mocha.

He stood at the alley entrance holding a bandage in one paw.

In the other, he held the broken mug's blue handle.

Essie stared.

He already knew.

"I broke it," she whispered. "And I lied. Latte didn't touch it."

The shadow shivered.

Then it slid down the buildings, across the pavement, and curled into its ordinary place beneath her paws.

Mocha knelt beside Essie.

"I wasn't angry about the mug," he said.

"I saw you holding your paw when you ran. I just wanted to make sure you were okay."

He wrapped her paw in the bandage.

Essie hugged him.

Then Latte appeared, still holding his ruined sandwich.

"Mocha? Did you eat my last fish pastry?"

Mocha blinked.

"Me? I've never eaten a pastry in my life."

Behind him, a second shadow climbed above the rooftops.
